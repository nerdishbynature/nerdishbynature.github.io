A road trip is a list of places in an order, and the driving between them. MyRoads keeps both in one place: add your stops, drag them into the order you want, and see the real distance and driving time for every leg as you go.

Planning

  • Search for any place and add it as a stop
  • Reorder stops and watch the routes recalculate
  • Real driving distance and travel time for every leg
  • Avoid tolls or motorways — for the whole trip, or one leg at a time
  • Running totals for the trip, on the map

Bring people along

  • Trips sync across your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account
  • Share a trip with whoever is coming, and let them edit it too
  • Send links and places into a trip from any other app, via the share sheet

Suggestions that stay on your device

MyRoads can write a short narrative of your route, suggest things worth stopping for near a place, and come up with a name for the trip. All of it runs on Apple’s on-device model — the text is generated on your iPhone or iPad, and nothing about your trip is sent to a server to do it. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the rest of the app works exactly the same.

Privacy

Your trips live on your device and in your own iCloud account. There is no server belonging to me, no account to create, and no analytics. Searching for a place and working out a route go to Apple’s mapping service, the same as in Maps. The privacy policy has the detail.
