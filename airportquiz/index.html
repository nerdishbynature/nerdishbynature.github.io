TXL, GRU, HND. If you have spent any time reading departure boards, you already know more of these than you think — and AirportQuiz is a fast way to find out how many.

Three ways to play

  • Normal. Fifteen questions, no clock, one score at the end.
  • Streak. Endless, with a timer on every question. One wrong answer ends the run.
  • Training. Untimed and unscored, for when you just want to learn the codes.

What is in it

  • 3,143 airports, from major hubs to Greenland heliports
  • Two Game Center leaderboards — best score and longest streak
  • Scores that fail to upload are queued and retried, so a tunnel does not cost you a run
  • Built in SwiftUI for current iOS, with no third-party code of any kind

Privacy

AirportQuiz has no server, no accounts and no analytics. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a score you choose to post to Game Center, which goes to Apple. Details are in the privacy policy.
